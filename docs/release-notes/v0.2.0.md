Daily-driver polish, real export & sharing, partial erasing, stylus shortcuts, and a 92% smaller APK.

### Highlights

- **Real Export & Share (PNG + SVG):** New Export tab in the document menu with a **Transparent background** toggle, **Save PNG to Photos** (`Pictures/Drawspace`), native Android **Share Sheet** for PNG & SVG, and **Save SVG…** via the system document picker.
- **Partial & Stroke Eraser Modes:** Switch between removing whole strokes/fills or carving only the touched portion of strokes and bucket fills, with a steady multi-buffered eraser ring cursor.
- **Pressure Eraser & Stylus Side Button:** Hold the stylus still and press firmly to erase until lift (with adjustable threshold), plus configurable stylus barrel/side-button actions (*Hold to Erase*, *Hold for Eyedropper*, *Press to Undo*, or *Off*).
- **Live Eyedropper Loupe & Multi-Finger Gestures:** Press and hold with one finger on the canvas to sample composited colors with a live split-ring loupe and hex readout; 2-finger tap Undo, 3-finger tap Redo, and 4-finger tap Zen mode.
- **Zen Mode & Left-Handed Layout:** Hide the canvas HUD and system bars for distraction-free sketching, and move the vertical tool capsule to the right edge in Settings for left-handed drawing.
- **Smoother Fast Strokes:** Stabilized stylus motion prediction during rapid movement.
- **~92% Smaller APK:** Enabled R8 code and resource shrinking, bringing the release APK down from ~50 MB to ~3.8 MB.
